Vibration Alarm

Arm it, set the phone down (on a door, a bag, a drawer, whatever you want to know was disturbed), and the accelerometer watches for a knock or a bump. The first one that crosses the threshold sets off a real, looping alarm sound -- a built-in siren, or any audio file you pick yourself -- and it keeps sounding until you silence it.

Not a security system. This only works while this tab stays open, the screen stays on (or a wake lock holds it), and the phone isn't locked or backgrounded -- mobile browsers throttle or fully suspend background tabs, which would silently stop detection. Don't rely on this for anything that actually matters.
