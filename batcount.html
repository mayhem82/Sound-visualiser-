Flying Fox Count

A counting aid for flying fox (bat) colony emergence and return, at sunset or sunrise. Point the camera at the sky — Outline mode redraws the whole feed as bright edge-lines on black, the same effect used elsewhere in this suite, which makes moving bats stand out as bright loops against the dark. Detection tracks each edge-blob frame to frame and only circles the ones actually moving in a direction, the way bats crossing the sky do, not static clutter like a tree or a wire junction. Work in progress: this does not reliably count for you — the tally you tap yourself is the count that matters.
