Selective Effects

Applies a real effect (cartoon posterize, or a duotone monochrome) to only the person in frame or only the background, using a real local person-segmentation model to decide where -- not a hand-drawn region, an actual pixel-by-pixel mask recomputed continuously from the live camera.

Standalone experiment, not part of Colour Vision Extreme. This explores the same "apply an effect only inside/outside a mask" idea as a self-contained page with its own much simpler effect engine, instead of risking changes to that page's own long-tuned correction pipeline.

Privacy Policy

Person model: not loaded Scene model: not loaded Object model: not loaded

The only feature in this whole suite with external dependencies. Every other page here is dependency-free; this one loads real pretrained models from a public CDN the first time each is needed -- that one-time fetch needs internet, cached by your browser after. Your camera video itself never leaves the device either way: all three models run entirely locally (WebAssembly/WebGL), the same as every other feature's local-only promise. "Person only"/"Background only" use a person-detector (MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation) -- a real but approximate mask, not a guaranteed cutout; poor lighting, unusual poses, more than one person, or being partly out of frame can all confuse it. "Sky only"/"Wall only" use a second, heavier general scene model (DeepLab, trained on ADE20K) that labels a frame into everyday classes like sky/wall/floor/tree -- a real semantic segmenter, not a lookup table, but coarser and slower than the person model. "AI: isolate a described object" and "Describe what the camera sees" use a third, heavier still model (OWL-ViT) that can locate open-ended short phrases you type rather than being limited to a fixed class list -- but it draws a rectangle around its best match, not a tight silhouette, and "Describe" only ever checks a fixed list of common object words against the frame, it doesn't freely caption a scene in its own words. All three re-detect fresh every cycle rather than tracking identity across frames -- real future work, not this page's job yet.
