Selective Effects

Applies a real effect (cartoon posterize, or a duotone monochrome) to only the person in frame or only the background, using a real local person-segmentation model to decide where -- not a hand-drawn region, an actual pixel-by-pixel mask recomputed continuously from the live camera.

Standalone experiment, not part of Colour Vision Extreme. This explores the same "apply an effect only inside/outside a mask" idea as a self-contained page with its own much simpler effect engine, instead of risking changes to that page's own long-tuned correction pipeline.

Privacy Policy

Segmentation model: not loaded

The only feature in this whole suite with an external dependency. Every other page here is dependency-free; this one loads a real pretrained person-segmentation model (MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation, a few MB) from a public CDN the first time you start it each session -- that one-time fetch needs internet, cached by your browser after. Your camera video itself never leaves the device either way: the model runs entirely locally (WebAssembly), the same as every other feature's local-only promise. The mask is a real but approximate person-detector, not a guaranteed cutout -- poor lighting, unusual poses, more than one person, or being partly out of frame can all confuse it. It only tells "person-shaped foreground" from "everything else"; it can't yet target one specific tapped/selected object, and it re-detects fresh every cycle rather than tracking a specific person's identity across frames -- both are real future work, not this page's job yet. Segmentation also runs on a throttled interval, not every rendered frame, to stay affordable, so the mask edge can lag slightly behind fast motion.
