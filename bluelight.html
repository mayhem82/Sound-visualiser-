Blue Light Filter

Reconstructs an approximate light spectrum from what the camera sees, runs it through a real blue-light-blocking filter's wavelength-by-wavelength transmission curve, then reprojects that filtered spectrum back to colour — working at the level of what a physical lens filter actually does to light, not just recolouring pixels.
