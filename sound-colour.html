Sound Colour

The correction engine from Colour Vision Extreme, dedicated to one question: what if sight and sound talked to each other? Sound → Colour: bass/mid/treble reach into the live correction itself (blend, spread, outline strength), on top of Audio colour tint's existing hue wash. Colour → Sound: the corrected view plays back as tone -- a melodic chime near a saved colour, an ambient drone from the scene's dominant hue, a filtered-noise texture from how busy/edgy the view is.
