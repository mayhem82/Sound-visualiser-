Video Call

Peer-to-peer video calling, direct between two devices — no account, no call server. Same room-code pairing as this suite's tablet-sharing feature, just two-way this time: both sides send camera and microphone.

Very restrictive networks (some corporate firewalls, certain mobile carrier setups) can still block a direct connection — there's a relay only for exchanging the initial handshake, not for the call itself.

No raw/original option — every style here transforms your image (posterize, edge lines, colour remap) before it's ever sent, so nobody on the other end can extract the unprocessed camera feed. Audio Colour Tint needs microphone access separately from the call's own audio, to read live bass/mid/treble and tint your video toward whichever's loudest.
