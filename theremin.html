Doppler Theremin

Plays a fixed, near-ultrasonic tone through the speaker and listens for its echo with the microphone -- a moving hand nearby shifts that echo's frequency (the Doppler effect), which drives a played pitch and volume in real time. A one-antenna analogue of a real theremin's proximity sensing, not a distance sensor.

Not precise sensing. This reads "something moved closer/further," never an exact distance -- results depend heavily on your device's speaker/mic and how quiet the room is. The probe tone sits right at the edge of human hearing and may be faintly audible, especially to younger listeners.
