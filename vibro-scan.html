Vibration Scan

Buzzes the phone's own vibration motor, then reads the accelerometer's response right after -- a rough, comparative way to spot a point that "rings" differently than its neighbours while you move the phone along something (a pipe, a panel, a beam).

Not a crack detector. The phone's vibration motor has no amplitude/frequency control and the accelerometer isn't a calibrated sensor -- how hard and how squarely you hold the phone against the surface affects the reading far more than a real defect would. Use this only to flag "this point looked different from the others nearby," never as a diagnosis.
