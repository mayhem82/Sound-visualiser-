Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026 (Video Production audio recording added)

This policy covers the Sound Visualiser app bundle: Sound Nebula, Property Colour Reference, and Video Production. (Colour Vision Extreme, Colour Assist, and their Viewer/Tutorials pages are a separate product with their own listing and privacy policy.) Everything below processes on your own device. There is no server, no account, and nothing described here is ever sent anywhere.

Camera

Every page in this bundle requests camera access to show and process the live video feed on screen. Video frames are processed in memory (colour correction, effects, particle visuals) and never leave the device.

Microphone

Sound Nebula uses the microphone to drive its audio-reactive particle visuals — audio is analysed in memory for volume/frequency and never recorded or stored. Video Production requests the microphone separately from the camera so it can record synchronised audio into your Takes; the audio is processed locally (volume, a low-cut filter for rumble, a warmer/brighter tone control, and a compressor) before it's captured, all via on-device audio nodes — none of it involves a server. Muting the Mic volume slider to 0%, or denying the microphone prompt, both still let you record video-only. Property Colour Reference doesn't use the microphone at all.

What's stored, and where

All saved data lives in your browser's local storage on your device, scoped to this site. Nothing syncs to a server or account:

  • Sound Nebula — your particle style and colour preferences
  • Property Colour Reference — saved calibration profiles and reference points
  • Video Production — Templates (recorded parameter automation), Cue Sheets, calibration points, and Take metadata (timestamps/trigger logs — not the video itself, see below)

Clearing your browser's site data for this app (or uninstalling it) permanently deletes all of it. There is no cloud backup and no way to recover it afterward.

Recorded video (Takes)

When Video Production records a Take, the resulting video (with audio, if the microphone was granted and Mic volume wasn't muted) is held in memory for that session only, so you can preview and download it. It is not automatically saved anywhere and does not survive a reload or app restart unless you explicitly download it first.

No analytics, no tracking, no third parties

The app makes no network requests of its own beyond loading its own pages, styles, and scripts. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting, and no third-party embed of any kind.

Permissions this app requests

  • Camera — required; every included page processes the live camera feed.
  • Microphone — used by Sound Nebula for its audio-reactive visuals, and by Video Production to record audio into Takes (optional — mute it with the Mic volume slider, or deny the prompt). Unused by Property Colour Reference.
  • Internet (Android package only) — required to load the app's own pages on first launch and after updates. No data is uploaded.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
